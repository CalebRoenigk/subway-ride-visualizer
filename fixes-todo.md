- Ridden Same Car Achivements (x1, x5, x10, etc)
- Ridden a car with and ID that has (2,3,4) numbers the same 
- Ridden all the cars in a trainset
- Ridden every trainset on a line
- Ridden each type of car
- Ridden Every car on a line
- Various badges for "funny" car numbers
